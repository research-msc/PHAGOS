PHAGOS

In Silico Discovery of Novel Candidate Acinetobacter baumannii Associated Bacteriophage Receptor-Binding Proteins

via Similarity-Aware Machine Learning, Independent External Validation, and Host-Unassigned Sequence Screening
Abstract
Carbapenem-resistant Acinetobacter baumannii is a critical antimicrobial-resistance threat, yet identifying bacteriophages with suitable host specificity remains labor-intensive. Receptor-binding proteins (RBPs), which mediate initial phage recognition of bacterial surfaces, may contain sequence signals useful for computational host-association screening. This study developed PHAGOS, a computer-based framework for prioritizing host-unassigned bacteriophage RBPs for possible A. baumannii association. PHAGOS used 886 unique known-host RBPs, including 64 A. baumannii-associated proteins, represented by nucleotide/codon, amino-acid, and physicochemical features under similarity-aware machine-learning evaluation. Random Forest and Logistic Regression models were compared with prevalence and BLASTp baselines and tested on an independent 1,378-protein external cohort. The nucleotide/codon Random Forest achieved the highest internal area under the precision-recall curve (AUPRC) of 0.7889. Feature integration did not show a statistically clear improvement, so the integration hypothesis was not supported. Externally, the integrated Random Forest achieved an AUPRC of 0.4771 versus 0.1421 for BLASTp. PHAGOS then screened a separate PhaRBP harvest beginning with 127,751 raw records and identified 20 metadata-vetted, sequence-dissimilar computational RBP candidates. PHAGOS therefore narrows large host-unassigned RBP collections into experimentally testable hypotheses rather than confirmed A. baumannii-infecting phages.

Methodology

1Dataset Curation

886 known-host RBPs; 64 A. baumannii positives.

2Feature Extraction

149 nucleotide/codon + 27 amino-acid + 9 physicochemical = 185 integrated features.

3Similarity Control

Primary 80% identity grouping; nested grouped cross-validation, 4 outer x 3 inner folds.

4Model Development

Logistic Regression, Random Forest, prevalence baseline, leakage-controlled BLASTp.

5Independent Validation

Frozen models tested on 1,378 known-host proteins without retraining.

6Discovery Screening

PhaRBP harvest metadata audit, CDS quality, labeled-reference similarity screen, CD-HIT80 redundancy control, frozen ranking, biological characterization.
